## 3. The 90-second query-response workflow

When a relevant query arrives in your inbox:

**Step 1 (10 sec):** Is the topic within our expertise? If no, archive. If yes, continue.

**Step 2 (30 sec):** Open the corresponding data source. Our most-cited data:
- **Municipal opt-in data:** `mainedispensaryguide.com/for-journalists` (embeddable widget + JSON)
- **Maine Cannabis Market Pulse 2026:** `mainedispensaryguide.com/market-pulse-2026`
- **Tax + 280E calculator:** `mainedispensaryguide.com/maine-cannabis-tax-calculator`
- **City guides (186+):** `mainedispensaryguide.com/guides/[city-name]-dispensary-guide`

**Step 3 (45 sec):** Compose the response. Template:

```
Hi [Reporter Name],

Great question. [One sentence direct answer with a specific data point
or fact from our research.]

For context: [2-3 sentences of relevant data, ideally with a specific
number from our research.]

MDG has the only public dataset of 34+ Maine opted-in municipalities,
plus a real-time embeddable widget and the 2026 Maine Cannabis Market
Pulse report. Both are free to use under CC BY 4.0 with attribution to
mainedispensaryguide.com.

Live data: [paste the most relevant URL]

Happy to follow up by phone or email if you need a quote, additional
data, or an interview with our team.

Best,
[Maine Dispensary Guide editorial]
press [at] mainedispensaryguide.com
```

**Step 4 (5 sec):** Always include 1-2 specific URLs to MDG resources. These become the backlinks.

**Rule of thumb:** respond to 1 of every 4-5 queries you receive. The volume is high but most queries aren't great fits; quality over quantity.

## 4. Sample query response (using existing data)

**Query:** "I'm writing a story about how Maine's municipal opt-in system has affected cannabis access. Are there statistics on which counties have the most opted-in towns?"

**Response:**

```

Yes. According to our 2026 Maine Cannabis Market Pulse, 34 of Maine's
488 municipalities have formally opted in to allow adult-use cannabis
businesses, with Cumberland County (Portland, Brunswick, Scarborough,
etc.) leading the most opted-in municipalities and Aroostook and
Piscataquis counties having only one each.

Specific data:
- Cumberland County: [N] opted-in municipalities (most)
- York County: [N]
- Kennebec County: [N]
- Penobscot County: [N]
- Androscoggin County: [N]
- Aroostook County: 1 (Presque Isle)
- Piscataquis County: 1 (Dover-Foxcroft)

The full dataset is published as a free embeddable widget at
mainedispensaryguide.com/embed/opt-in-tracker and as JSON at
mainedispensaryguide.com/data/maine-opt-in-towns.json — both are free
to use under CC BY 4.0 with attribution.

Happy to provide additional context or an interview with our team.
Best,
[editorial]
```

**Result:** The article will link to the widget URL, the JSON, and the Market Pulse. **Three backlinks from one response.**

## 6. Quarterly refresh

Every 90 days, refresh the boilerplate with new data points. The most powerful queries to respond to are the ones where you have a specific data point the journalist needs. Every quarter, add 2-3 new data points to the boilerplate:

- Q3 2026: Add new opt-in towns (refresh Opt-In Tracker)
- Q4 2026: Add 2026 sales totals from OCP
- Q1 2027: Add caregiver market size update
- Q2 2027: Add 2027 legislative session (LD bills) data

This is a 30-min task per quarter that keeps the boilerplate current and increases response-to-publication rate.
