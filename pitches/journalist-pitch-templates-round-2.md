## Why this round-2 list is shorter

Round-1 had 15 contacts. Round-2 has 5 confirmed Tier-A resends
plus up to 4 more PENDING-RESEARCH slots once verified human
contacts land. The math: 9 surviving live-human targets, minus
the 4 we still need to find a real contact for, equals ~5-9
real sends in round 2.

That's intentional. The 2026 cold-outreach benchmarks (Salesmotion,
Autobound, Snov.io, Belkins) put reply rates at 5-9% for
basic-personalization (first name + publication name) and
15-25% for signal-personalized (specific trigger event + tailored
value prop). 5-9 highly-targeted sends at 15-25% = 1-3 replies.
15 generic sends at 5-9% = 1 reply. The signal-personalized
smaller list is a better expected outcome with less brand risk.
