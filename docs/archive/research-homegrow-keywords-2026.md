## Caveats / Things I Couldn't Verify

- **No real search-volume data was accessible from this environment.** The keyword list above is built on SERP analysis (i.e., queries I confirmed return Maine-relevant results), not on volume curves. Phase 2 should validate volume with Ahrefs, Semrush, or Google Keyword Planner. Several queries I expected to find (e.g., "Maine home grow reddit") returned no results — may be too low-volume to be worth targeting.
- **Leafly's Maine plant count is still listed as 3 mature in their `/learn/legalization/maine` page.** This is a confirmed opportunity (outdated, high-authority, easy to outrank with our correct post-LD 555 content) but I didn't capture a current timestamp on that page. Worth re-checking before publishing the comparison table.
- **Maine seed bank affiliate programs:** I could not confirm direct affiliate program availability for North Atlantic Seed Co. (Maine-licensed) or Seed & Soil (Humboldt Seed Co. affiliate). The user mentioned ILGM 20% / MSNL up to 35% / Seed Connect 20-30%. The first two are confirmed. Seed Connect's "20-30%" needs verification — I could not find a public affiliate-program page in my searches. Worth a quick check before Phase 2.
- **Federal legal status update (Nov 2025 hemp definition change):** Barney's Farm's "Home Grow Laws by State 2026" post mentions a November 12, 2026 federal change that reclassifies high-THC seeds as controlled substances. If accurate, this is a major story for Maine growers who buy seeds online and would warrant its own post. I could not independently verify this from a primary source within the time available — flag for editorial review.
- **Competitor page quality was judged by visible content and authority signals, not by their actual keyword rankings.** I confirmed they rank for the head terms I searched, but full SERP overlap with the mainedispensaryguide.com long-tail list would need a dedicated tool.
