# Resource-Page Link Prospecting — Framework & Initial List

**Date prepared:** 2026-07-07
**Mission:** Find 30 curated "best of" / "resources" pages on authority sites that
list state cannabis guides (NJ, FL, MI, MA, IL, etc.) but NOT MDG. Send a
personalized pitch asking to be added.

**Why this works:** Resource pages on authority sites (industry trade publications,
cannabis lawyer/CPA sites, .edu programs, .org cannabis associations) routinely link
to other state cannabis guides. Maine is currently absent from these lists. A simple
"Hey, you list the NJ/FL/MA guides — here's the Maine equivalent, would you add us?"
email converts at 5-15%.

**Time per pitch:** 1-2 hours (find page, verify it's relevant, send personalized email).

## How to find prospects

### Search operators (run these in Google)

```
# General "best of" pages
intitle:"best cannabis" intitle:"resources" cannabis OR marijuana
intitle:"cannabis resources" intitle:"links"
inurl:resources intitle:cannabis

# State-specific
"new jersey cannabis guide" OR "nj cannabis guide" intitle:resources
"michigan cannabis" intitle:resources intitle:links
"illinois cannabis" intitle:resources intitle:links
"california cannabis" intitle:resources intitle:links

# Trade associations
site:.org cannabis intitle:resources
site:.edu cannabis OR marijuana intitle:resources
site:*.org intitle:"state cannabis resources"

# Lawyer / CPA / Consultant sites
inurl:resources intitle:cannabis inurl:cannabis-business
inurl:links intitle:cannabis lawyer OR attorney
```

### Targets to look for (publication types that link out)

1. **State cannabis trade associations** — e.g. Maine Cannabis Industry Association (if it exists)
2. **Cannabis lawyer / CPA firm resource pages** — e.g. Vicente Sederberg, Frost Brown Todd, etc.
3. **Cannabis media resource pages** — e.g. MJBizDaily, CBT, Ganjapreneur
4. **Cannabis conferences** — e.g. MJBizCon, NECANN
5. **Cannabis education sites** — e.g. Cannabis Training Institute, Green CulturED
6. **State tourism boards** — Visit Maine, Maine Office of Tourism
7. **Regional press resource pages** — e.g. Portland Press Herald, Mainebiz
8. **B2B directories** — Crunchbase, Cannabiz Media directory
9. **Government / quasi-government resource pages** — Small Business Administration state chapters, state economic development
10. **University cannabis programs** — few exist, but where they do (e.g. UC Boulder), they often link out

## Why this works (the strategic logic)

The comparative analysis (see `docs/2026-07-07-backlink-comparative-analysis.md`)
ranked resource page link building as the **#2 highest-ROI channel** for niche sites
like MDG, behind linkable assets but ahead of digital PR. The math:

- Time per pitch: 1-2 hours
- Acceptance rate (industry avg): 5-15%
- Realistic links per month: 2-4 (sending 30-50 pitches)
- Effort: 30-50 hours / month
- Realistic annual output: 25-50 links

The trade-off vs. digital PR: digital PR gets higher DR (MJBizDaily DR 76) but
slower turnaround (3-6 weeks for an article to publish). Resource page links are
DR 30-70 (mixed) but appear in 1-2 weeks. For Moz DR, both move the needle;
resource pages compound faster.

**If the operator can send 5 pitches per day for 2 weeks, that's 50 pitches. At
10% acceptance, that's 5 links in 2 weeks. In 3 months, that's 30+ links.**

The work is mechanical. The hardest part is finding the prospects — which is what
this document is for.
