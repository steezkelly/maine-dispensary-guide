## Maine-specific moat (no competitor publishes this)

1. **CMP Time-of-Use rate, effective January 1, 2026.** Off-peak delivery rate $0.067/kWh vs. on-peak $0.503/kWh (5-9 PM weekdays). A Maine indoor grower who schedules lights 9 PM-9 AM pays zero on-peak delivery charges. Source: cmpco.com/time-of-use-delivery-rate. **This is the page's headline data point and a content moat no national grow-guide site has localized.**

2. **Maine water hardness (6-17 ppm in coastal population centers).** Most Maine home growers can skip reverse-osmosis filtration entirely. The "Maine indoor grower advantage." Source: waterhardness.org/state/maine.

3. **Maine winter basement grow advantage.** Year-round 50-60°F basement temperatures, no summer cooling costs (vs. Texas/Florida/Arizona growers paying hundreds/month on AC). The "Maine basement is ideal for indoor" framing.

4. **Maine-specific storm-outage planning.** Ice storm 2023/2024 outage data for CMP/Versant. UPS/Jackery/generator recommendations sized for Maine winter.

5. **Coast of Maine Stonington Blend Grower's Mix.** The only major cannabis-formulated soil brand manufactured in Maine. OMRI-listed, lobster/crab shell meal + kelp + mycorrhizae. A genuine local recommendation no national grow-guide can make.

## Risk surface

- **Versant residential rate is an estimate** (~$0.22-0.25/kWh). The page marks this clearly with an "(estimate)" disclaimer. A future research cycle should confirm at versantpower.com.
- **CMP standard Rate A combined rate** ($0.136/kWh) is derived from EIA Maine data, not directly scraped from a live CMP bill. The TOU rate (the headline data point) is directly sourced and verified.
- **Maine dispensary retail price** ($200-400/oz) is reused from the autoflower-vs-feminized brief and should be re-verified against live dispensary menu prices. The page flags this in the disclaimer.
- **Maine water hardness for specific cities** (Augusta at 150 ppm, Belfast at 68 ppm) is sourced from waterhardness.org and Aquatell. The Portland/Bangor/Lewiston numbers match Portland Water District public data. Augusta is the standout hard-water city and is flagged in the table.
- **Equipment prices fluctuate** — the page uses ranges (e.g., "Vivosun S448 4×4: $80-120") rather than single prices to be honest about retailer variance.
