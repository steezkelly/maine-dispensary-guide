## Top 3 to Pitch First (and why)

**Pitch #1: Bangor Daily News (OpEd to Susan Young).** This is the highest-probability, highest-ROI play. BDN has a named, accessible opinion editor who publicly invited Maine-focused commentary in 2025, and they explicitly encourage hyperlinks to sources — meaning mainedispensaryguide.com gets an in-content editorial link (not just an author bio). The 650-word column is the right length, the editorial calendar favors timely local policy, and the pitch (municipal opt-in data + 500ft buffer analysis) is concrete enough that an editor can immediately see the page. Realistic acceptance: HIGH; time to publish: 2-4 weeks.

**Pitch #2: The Bollard (Chris Busby, [email protected]).** The Bollard is the only publication on this list that has actively run adversarial coverage of the Maine Office of Cannabis Policy — the "Dear Vern" open letter from April 2024 establishes a clear editorial lane. A pitch framed as a follow-up to that coverage (the OCP's enforcement gaps, the "gray market" persistence) will land with a small, opinionated alt-weekly editor who is starved for substantive, sourced submissions. The Bollard links liberally and uses author bios with URLs, so a contextual link in a 1,200-word feature is highly achievable. Time to publish: 1-3 weeks.

**Pitch #3: Cannabis Industry Journal (Aaron Biros, editor).** CIJ is the highest-value trade-publication fit on topical match (compliance, dispensary operations, cultivation) and has the most generous submission workflow (editor pre-consultation, 2-3 week review, 1200-2000 words, dofollow editorial links). A piece on Maine's three-tier cannabis system (adult-use vs. medical vs. hemp labeling/testing) is a strong evergreen compliance angle that any multi-state operator would bookmark and link to internally — amplifying the backlink's downstream value beyond just the DR. Time to publish: 6-10 weeks. Critical: contact Aaron Biros BEFORE writing per their explicit guidelines.

**Sequence: Bollard first (fastest turnaround, builds confidence), BDN second (highest DR + named editor), CIJ third (highest trade-publication authority). Skip Ganjapreneur and CBT initially — their editorial bar is higher and they receive hundreds of pitches; deprioritize Leafly, High Times, Down East, and Maine Monitor unless one of the first three converts.**
