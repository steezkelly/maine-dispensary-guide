## The two growth levers (ranked by impact)

### Lever 1: Backlink-driven DR growth → ranks climb → organic sessions grow

**Why this is the primary lever:**
- Backlinks from DR 60+ sources (Bollard DR45, BDN OpEd DR78, Cannabis Industry Journal DR55) directly increase the site's authority, which lifts ranking positions on all 61 existing pages
- A single BDN OpEd could plausibly move the home-grow pillar from page-3 to page-1 for "Maine home grow cannabis" queries, which would push GA4 sessions past 500/mo within 2-3 months
- No social accounts required; no follower count to defend; works as long as the content is good

**What an agent can do autonomously:**
- Draft the Bollard pitch (long-form OCP policy critique, Maine-specific, 800-1200 words) — high-fit content, written from existing site data
- Draft the BDN OpEd pitch (Maine opt-in map, 600-800 words) — high-fit content
- Draft the Cannabis Industry Journal pitch (Maine 3-tier labeling rules, 1200-2000 words) — high-fit content

**What requires Steve:**
- The actual email send. Subagents can draft, but a freelance-style pitch email from "Maine Dispensary Guide editorial team" needs to come from a real person at the publishing site's listed contact email
- The actual writing of the underlying article (subagents can also draft, but the byline credibility is on Steve — he's the editorial lead)
- Response follow-up if the editor replies

**Realistic timeline:** First pitch sent within 1 week, first backlink landed within 2-6 weeks, organic uplift visible in GA4 within 60-90 days.

### Lever 2: Direct guest-content publishing on third-party sites (no-pitch strategy)

**Why this is secondary:**
- Requires the same social/traffic signal as the affiliate programs
- Less compounding than DR uplift (a single article on a third-party site ranks for the topic, but doesn't lift authority across the domain)
- But it's a faster feedback loop: write, publish, see referral traffic

**What an agent can do autonomously:**
- Substack / Medium cross-posts of existing MDG content with canonical links
- LinkedIn long-form posts by Steve (rewrites of MDG article sections)
- Forum engagement (r/MEtrees, GardenWeb, Rollitup — but with nofollow risk; only for traffic, not DR)

**What requires Steve:**
- Account creation (Substack is fast; LinkedIn is fast; Reddit has karma gates)
- The LinkedIn identity / Substack identity — needs to be Steve's voice and brand
