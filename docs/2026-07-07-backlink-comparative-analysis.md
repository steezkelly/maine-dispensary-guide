## TL;DR

MDG has a **mature outreach plan** (3 docs, 14+ named targets, real contacts). The plan is well-designed but **stuck in planning** — every "Date sent" row is blank. The bottleneck isn't *finding* link prospects; it's **(a) executing the existing pitches, (b) manufacturing the linkable assets that make pitches convert, and (c) supplementing with the 3-4 acquisition channels the current plan doesn't emphasize**.

The most cost-effective DR-yield per hour-of-work: **1. turn existing data into a press kit journalists can cite, 2. publish 2-3 original-research linkable assets, 3. execute the existing journalist pitches**. The current plan has Tier 1-4 named targets but lacks the "linkable asset production line" that powers Tier 1's conversion.

## Recommended new linkable assets (3 highest ROI for MDG)

These would each earn passive links for years. Build in priority order.

### Asset 1: "Maine Cannabis Opt-In Tracker" — public, embeddable, monthly-updated

**What it is:** A live, embeddable widget showing the 41+ Maine towns that have opted in vs. not. The data already exists at `/guides/maine-cannabis-opt-in-tracker.astro`. The new layer is: (a) the data is published as JSON for embedding, (b) there's an embeddable `<iframe>` snippet, (c) it's auto-updated monthly.

**Why it earns links:**
- Journalists covering Maine cannabis policy need *the* current data
- 200+ searches/month for "how to open a dispensary in maine" / "maine cannabis opt in" (GSC) — high search demand for a reference page
- Other state cannabis sites (NJ, FL, MI, MA guides per Tier 2 in current plan) will cite the methodology and link back
- Trade press (CBT, MJBizDaily) will cite as primary source

**Effort:** 2-3 days. The data exists; the work is just publishing it as embeddable data + an embed snippet page.

**DR-yield per year:** 10-25 referring domains (per industry data: original research pages earn 3.2× baseline).

### Asset 2: "Cannabis Tax Calculator" + "Maine 280E Cost Scenario Builder"

**What it is:** A two-part calculator: (a) Maine excise tax (per 2024 statute) on any retail price, (b) 280E cost analysis for a cultivator scenario. The 280E piece is **unique to MDG** — no other Maine cannabis site has this depth, and it's the kind of thing a CPA or attorney writing a cannabis tax article would cite.

**Why it earns links:**
- "Cannabis tax calculator" + "280E" queries are persistent evergreen (not seasonal)
- Cannabis CPAs and tax attorneys are the natural linkers — they cite tools in client advisories
- Existing `roi-calculator.astro` is undermarketed — same play, but specific to tax

**Effort:** 3-4 days (more complex tax math). Reuse the existing ROI calculator's UI.

**DR-yield per year:** 15-30 referring domains.

### Asset 3: "Maine Cannabis Market Pulse" — annual data report

**What it is:** A published annual report aggregating MDG's GSC search analytics (355KB of data) + opt-in tracker data + scraped public data. The format: PDF + interactive web page with charts, methodology, raw data download. Title: "Maine Cannabis Market Pulse 2026" or "Maine's 41-Town Reality: 2026 Annual Report on Municipal Opt-Ins and Operator Economics."

**Why it earns links:**
- **The data already exists.** The 355KB GSC search analytics + the 2 audit reports + the opt-in tracker data are sitting in `data/`. The work is to *aggregate, format, publish*.
- Journalists covering Maine cannabis need *current data*. They cite original research over opinion by 3.2× (Backlinko 2026).
- B2B cannabis brands (CPAs, attorneys, consultants, real estate brokers) will cite in client materials.
- Industry press (CBT, MJBizDaily, Ganjapreneur) explicitly commissions "State of the Industry" features per the existing outreach plan.

**Effort:** 1-2 weeks. Mostly synthesis and chart design.

**DR-yield per year:** 20-50 referring domains (the single highest-yield asset).

## Recommended new acquisition channels (3 missing from current plan)

### Channel 1: Resource page link building

**What it is:** Find curated "best of" pages on authority sites that already exist (Maine Business Review, Cannabis industry resource pages, college cannabis programs, etc.) and request inclusion.

**How it works:** Search operators like:
- `cannabis resources intitle:"resources"`
- `maine business resources inurl:resources`
- `intitle:"best of" cannabis education`
- `intitle:"resources" cannabis compliance`

When you find a page that links to competitor guides but not MDG, email the editor: *"We noticed you list NJ Cannabis Guide / FL Cannabis Guide on your resources page. We're the Maine equivalent, would you consider adding us as well? We focus on regulatory/B2B content for Maine operators."*

**Effort:** 1-2 hours per pitch, 30-50 prospects per month, 5-15% acceptance rate.

**Why MDG should do this:** The MDG site has 2 dedicated resource pages (`/resources` and `/resources/maine-cannabis-official-resources`). These make MDG a candidate to be added to *other* sites' resource pages — natural symmetry. Many other state guides do the same.

### Channel 2: Broken link reclamation

**What it is:** Find authority sites with dead outbound links, offer your page as a replacement.

**How to find them:** Use tools like Ahrefs Content Explorer (paid), or free techniques: `site:maine.gov inurl:resources "cannabis"` and check for 404s, then email the site owner: *"Noticed your resources page links to X which is now broken — we have a similar guide on the same topic at mainedispensaryguide.com/Y. Would you consider updating the link?"*

**Targets in Maine cannabis ecosystem:**
- Maine.gov pages (very high DR)
- University of Maine pages (DR 90+)
- Maine Municipal Association resources
- OCP / Metrc partner pages

**Effort:** 3-4 hours per successful replacement (research, find candidates, write personalized email, follow up).

**DR-yield per link:** Very high (gov/.edu links are 80+ DR typically). 5-10 link acquisitions per quarter is realistic.

### Channel 3: HARO-equivalent signups (passive inbound)

**What it is:** Sign up for free journalist query services and respond to relevant queries within 24 hours.

**Services:**
- **Featured.com** (formerly HARO) — $0 tier + paid tier; queries go to email
- **Qwoted** — free tier available
- **PressWhizz** — free tier
- **Terkel** — free tier
- **SourceBottle** — free tier

**Setup:** 30 minutes to register with a strong MDG boilerplate ("Maine Dispensary Guide — operator of mainedispensaryguide.com, 186 Maine cannabis guides, original municipal opt-in data, 1,554-caregiver market analysis available on request").

**Ongoing:** 5-10 minutes per relevant query. Expect 1-3 links per month from these once you're established.

**Why MDG should do this:** The MDG site has the *exact* data journalists covering Maine cannabis need (municipal opt-ins, 280E tax, caregiver market, 41-town analysis). Signups take 30 min and pay for themselves within 1-2 successful queries.

## What's actually broken in the existing plan

Reading the three existing outreach docs together:

1. **The plan is asset-poor.** It tells journalists "we have data" but doesn't point them to specific data products they can cite. Every pitch is "pitch Maine as a story" — the journalist has to do work to verify. Pre-built data products invert the loop: "Here's our annual report; here's the embeddable data; here's the live tracker — feel free to use it."

2. **The plan is execution-poor.** All 13 outreach rows show "NOT SENT" as of 2026-07-04. Three weeks have passed since the tracker started. The pitches exist; nobody's hit send.

3. **The plan is channel-narrow.** It only covers editorial outreach and resource listings. Missing: linkable assets, resource page prospecting, broken link reclamation, HARO-equivalent signups.

4. **The plan has a public-side gap.** No press kit, no embeddable widgets, no "for journalists" page. Every pitch is asking the journalist to do extra work.

The fix isn't "add more prospects to the outreach list." The fix is **invert the loop** — build the linkable assets, publish the press kit, and the pitches convert 5-10× better because the journalist's first reaction is "oh, they have a widget I can embed" not "let me check if this is real."
