# Maine Dispensary Guide — National/Federal Cannabis Market Source Pack

**Purpose:** Editorial source pack for the MDG `/market-stats` page national-perspective expansion. Author pulls facts from this brief; do not synthesize beyond what's in the source. This brief covers 8 federal/national data domains the existing market-stats.astro page cites only sparingly (4 BDSA + 3 MJBiz + 1 Census mentions vs. dense state-OCP coverage).

**Access date for all sources:** 2026-07-09 (today).

**Author caveats (YMYL + primary-source discipline):**

1. The **$8.92/g national retail flower price** cited in the existing market-stats.astro does not appear in BDSA's public-facing pricing pages (bdsa.com/solutions/cannabis-market-pricing-data/) — it traces to **Cannabis Benchmarks 2025** (per cannabispromotions.com/stats/prices, last verified 2026-06-29). The most recent direct primary tracker (Headset, June 2026) reports **$8.94/g for single-gram packages** and **$3.62/g equivalized across all package sizes**. The brief flags both.
2. The **Leafly Jobs Report 2026** figures circulate via cannabispromotions.com/stats/employment and the Vangst Jobs Report 2025; Leafly's own canonical post URL is `leafly.com/news/industry/cannabis-jobs-report` (last directly-canonical update was the 2022 edition at 428,059 jobs; the 2025/2026 numbers are cited by aggregators). Authors should treat the 445,800 figure as **cited-via-aggregator** unless they pull Leafly's 2026 PDF directly.
3. **DEA scheduling** changed materially between 2026-04-22 and 2026-07-09. As of 2026-07-09, **FDA-approved marijuana products + state-licensed medical marijuana are Schedule III** (effective 2026-04-22); broader rescheduling (all marijuana including adult-use) is **pending** the DEA ALJ hearing that commenced 2026-06-29. Adult-use cannabis is **still Schedule I** at the federal level.
4. **NSDUH estimates from 2021 onward are NOT directly comparable to 2020 and earlier** (SAMHSA methodological note in the 2024 release: multimode data collection methodology change). Trend claims must respect this.
5. **FBI UCR marijuana arrest data is incomplete** (NORML, 2024-09-25: "in 2022, 17 percent of agencies — representing 25 percent of the total US population — failed to report crime data to the FBI"). NORML's 217,150 figure is a **floor, not a ceiling**.
