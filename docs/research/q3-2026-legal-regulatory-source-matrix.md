# Q3 2026 Maine Cannabis Legal & Regulatory Source Matrix

**Prepared:** 2026-07-22  
**Temporal anchor:** legal/regulatory position as of 2026-07-22 (Q3 2026 opening; before the scheduled July 29 effective-date cluster and before the August 3/14 testing-rulemaking milestones).  
**Jurisdiction:** Maine; adult-use cannabis, medical cannabis, testing, taxes, recalls/advisories, and 132nd Legislature developments.  
**Use:** editorial/report planning; not legal advice.

## Executive status summary

- **Operative now:** the final adult-use rules remain 18-691 CMR chapters 5 and 40 (Chapter 5 effective 2024-11-12; Chapter 40 effective 2024-11-06), read with Title 28-B and later enacted laws. P.L. 2025, ch. 764 (LD 1488) is an emergency law effective 2026-04-19 and changes the treatment of concentrate-made edible products.
- **Enacted but not yet operative on the temporal anchor:** P.L. 2025, chapters 504, 506, 512, 514, and 611 are scheduled by OCP for 2026-07-29 unless a specific implementation condition applies. Do not write these as already effective on 2026-07-22.
- **Proposed, not binding:** OCP's 2026 routine-technical drafts for chapters 5 and 40 were posted 2026-07-15. The hearing is 2026-08-03 at 10:00 a.m.; comments close 2026-08-14 at 5:00 p.m. OCP says final adoption will occur after comment review and the rules will take effect five days after adoption. No adoption/effective-date notice was located in the official sources reviewed for this matrix.
- **Important source-state warning:** the online Revisor statute pages visibly lag some January/April 2026 chapter laws (for example, Title 28-B §§605 and 2423-A pages show older extraction dates and do not yet display the 2026 amendments). For Q3 changes, cite the official chapter law and OCP implementation guidance first, then use codified text as a checked baseline with a lag note.
- **Safety evidence:** OCP's Q2 2026 adult-use testing report, updated 2026-07-15, reports 207 failing samples out of 4,490 (4.61%); retests are excluded. The official recall/advisory index lists four 2026 incidents through 2026-06-16: MarijuanaVille, Northwoods Caregiver, Green Trap, and Klouds Society/ChoicesLab.

## Source hierarchy used

1. Official chapter/session law and Maine Legislature bill disposition.
2. Current final rule on OCP's rules page and the rule PDF itself.
3. OCP formal implementation guidance, rulemaking notice, and current data pages.
4. Maine Revenue Services bulletin and current Title 36 text for tax implementation.
5. Legislature bill records for proposed, failed, withdrawn, or dead proposals.

Search snippets and third-party summaries were used only to locate official URLs. They are not evidence in the matrix.

### Table footnotes to include

1. **As-of date:** 2026-07-22.  
2. **“Current” means operative law/final rule/guidance on that date; “enacted” means passed but not necessarily effective; “proposed” means draft under comment; “failed/withdrawn” means no current legal effect.**  
3. Current final rules and the enacted 2026 statute should be read together; where the codified Revisor page lags, cite the chapter law and OCP guidance.  
4. OCP guidance is implementation guidance, not a substitute for the statute or rule and not legal advice.  
5. For draft rule text, quote only after visual redline inspection; extracted PDF text may contain both additions and deletions.

## Suggested report architecture

1. **At-a-glance:** a three-state strip: operative now / enacted future-effective / proposed or failed.
2. **Q3 timeline:** use the candidate dates above; visually distinguish effective dates from enactment, guidance, hearing, and comment deadlines.
3. **Current-vs-proposed testing table:** lead with S4 (current statutory edible exception), then S1/S3 (current final rules), then S5-S7 (draft rulemaking).
4. **Operator impact box:** taxes (S8-S10), medical trade shows/outdoor cultivation/background checks (S11-S14), with July 29 implementation caveats.
5. **Safety and oversight box:** OCP Q2 testing data plus the four 2026 recall/advisory rows (S17-S19). Separate adult-use recall authority from medical advisory authority.
6. **Not law / policy watch box:** LD 1847, LD 1365, LD 1983, LD 1620/1820. State the disposition and avoid implying a pending bill is a current requirement.
7. **Source notes:** direct official URLs, printed PDF page/section pinpoints, retrieval date, and a “last recheck” field.
