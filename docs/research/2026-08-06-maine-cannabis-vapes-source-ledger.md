# Maine cannabis vapes — source ledger

Review date: 2026-08-06

Scope: consumer-facing guidance about **adult-use** cannabis vapes sold in Maine. This is not a medical-use label guide, product recommendation, dosing guide, or clinical safety assessment.

| Claim class | Source and status | Safe editorial wording | Do not claim |

| Package controls | §701(2) | Adult-use products must be prepackaged in, or placed at final sale in, child-resistant and tamper-evident packaging as applicable. | Package controls make a product safe for every person or prevent all accidents. |
| Mandatory testing | [28-B M.R.S. §602](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/28-B/title28-Bsec602.html), current statute page reviewed 2026-08-06 | A licensee may not sell or distribute an adult-use product unless required testing shows it does not exceed the applicable maximum contamination levels. ## Page-wide wording rules

- Say “traceable” only to mean that a buyer can identify the batch and applicable regulated-product information, including recall notices. Do not use it as a safety guarantee.
- Attribute labels and testing to adult-use requirements; distinguish statute, current rule, OCP notice, and public-health context.
- No dosage, titration, onset, efficacy, symptom-management, or individualized product-selection advice.
- Present exact recall information as a dated historical example and link the primary OCP notice.
- Where a reader has a health concern or emergency, do not diagnose; link or direct only to the relevant public-health/emergency authority in the source context.
