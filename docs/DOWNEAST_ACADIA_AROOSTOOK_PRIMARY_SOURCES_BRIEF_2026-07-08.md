### Fact pulls (2–3 sentences each, as printed on the OCP page)

- OCP describes the dataset as a roster updated on Mondays (Tuesday if Monday is a state/federal holiday); the Licensee Search page (captured 2026-07-08) states the latest inline files were "updated July 8, 2026" with the May 2026 snapshot.
